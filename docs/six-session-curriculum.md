# The Six-Session Series

The summit workshop is session 4. The full series takes a creator from an empty laptop to a portfolio piece that passes the 7-point anti-slop audit.

| Number | Session | You leave with | Starter-kit folders |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Local studio | A working offline stack, a passing `check_setup.py`, and an understanding of model licenses. | `environment/` |
| 2 | Pre-production agents | A sourced research log, a director brief with exclusions, and a project bible, all validated by schema. | `schemas/`, `templates/`, `pipelines/01-pre-production/` |
| 3 | Media pipelines | Grey-box previs in Blender, depth passes, and keyframes from a ComfyUI graph you can read node by node. | `pipelines/02-production/` |
| 4 | Agentic workflows (the summit) | Agent cards, deterministic checks, human gates and the 60-minute team build. | `agents/`, `workshop-build/` |
| 5 | Post and QC | Whisper captions checked by a person, an OTIO timeline cut in Kdenlive, loudness and delivery QC, and a provenance ledger. | `pipelines/03-post-production/` |
| 6 | Capstone | An end-to-end piece of your own, scored on all seven anti-slop checks and signed by you. | All of it |

## Session details

### 1. Local studio

Hardware tiers and what each can run. Installing Ollama, Blender, Kdenlive, GIMP and ComfyUI. Free versus open-source versus open-weight versus local. Running a model with no network.

### 2. Pre-production agents

Writing a research log where every claim has a source. The director's three questions and the exclusion list. Building a bible with visual locks. Why the model gets a schema, not a conversation.

### 3. Media pipelines

Framing and focal length as numbers. Blocking in grey boxes. Depth passes and ControlNet. Seeds, takes and why you keep the rejects.

### 4. Agentic workflows

One agent, one job. Declared tools and forbidden actions. Machine checks versus human judgement. Gates, Git and the four exits. The live build.

### 5. Post and QC

Recording a clean voiceover on a phone. Whisper and why a person still reads every caption. OpenTimelineIO into Kdenlive. Loudness, true peak and delivery specs. Open and closed captions.

### 6. Capstone

Pick your own client or story. Run the full pipeline. Present the cut, the audit, the provenance ledger and one minute on where a human made it better.
