# Project Brief: Ember & Oak Launch

Client: Ember & Oak Coffee

## 1. Director's three answers

- **Who is it for?** Neighbors who already walk past the new shop on their way to work
- **What should they feel?** That the roastery down the street is part of their morning, not a chain
- **What should they do next?** Stop in at the second shop this week

## 2. Key message

Small batches roasted in our back room, every bag dated, and now a short walk closer.

## 3. Runtime

30 seconds.

## 4. Tone

Grounded, Warm, Communal

## 5. Exclusions

This spot will never:

1. No slow-motion latte-art pour
2. No stock hipster-barista tropes or forced smiles
3. No claims we cannot source, such as best coffee in town

## 6. References

- Dziga Vertov, Man with a Movie Camera (1929): a city waking up, observed rather than staged
- Morning window light in documentary still photography: one source, honest shadows
- Room tone of a working roastery: drum rumble, beans cracking, a door bell
