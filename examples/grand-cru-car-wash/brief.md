# Project Brief: The Sommelier of Suds

Client: Grand Cru Car Wash

## 1. Director's three answers

- **Who is it for?** Temecula Valley drivers whose cars wear a permanent layer of vineyard dust
- **What should they feel?** Amused that a car wash talks like a tasting room, and charmed enough to try it
- **What should they do next?** Book a Reserve Triple Foam this weekend

## 2. Key message

Three vintages, from House Rinse to Grand Cru Ceramic, each finished by hand. Now pouring in the Temecula Valley.

## 3. Runtime

30 seconds.

## 4. Tone

Deadpan, Mock-luxurious, Affectionate, Dry

## 5. Exclusions

This spot will never:

1. No naming or mocking real wineries or businesses
2. No water-waste or drought jokes
3. No bikini car-wash tropes

## 6. References

- Wes Anderson's The Grand Budapest Hotel (2014): symmetrical frames and deadpan formality
- Alexander Payne's Sideways (2004): earnest wine-tasting language, played completely straight
- Luxury car commercials: slow sliding shots over wet, gleaming paint
