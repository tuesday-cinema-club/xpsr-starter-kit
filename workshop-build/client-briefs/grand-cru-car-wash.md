# Client Brief C: Grand Cru Car Wash (Ironic)

> Workshop practice client. Grand Cru Car Wash is a fictional business written for this exercise. Temecula Valley is a real place; the two facts about it below come from public sources listed in the research log.

## The ask

Grand Cru Car Wash is opening in the Temecula Valley, Southern California's wine country. They want a **30-second ironic launch spot**: a car wash that talks about itself like a tasting room.

## The joke, and who it is on

The joke is on the car wash taking itself far too seriously: wash packages described like wine flights, an attendant dressed as a sommelier, foam swirled in a glass. It is never on Temecula, its wineries, or the people who live there.

## Tone words from the client

Deadpan, mock-luxurious, affectionate, dry.

## What the director decides at Gate 01

1. **Who is it for?**
2. **What should they feel?**
3. **What should they do next?**

The room then adds three exclusions. Strong suggestions for this brief: no naming or mocking real wineries or businesses, and no water-waste or drought jokes.
