# Client Brief B: Spoke Co-op

> Workshop practice client. Spoke Co-op is a fictional organization written for this exercise.

## The ask

Spoke is a volunteer-run bike-repair co-op recruiting new members. They want a **30-second recruitment spot** for community boards and social media.

## Client fact sheet

Treat these as the only facts you can claim. Add each one to your `research_log.csv` before you cite it.

| Suggested id | Fact |
| --- | --- |
| SRC-001 | Volunteers teach members to repair their own bikes; they do not repair bikes for you. |
| SRC-002 | Members can use the shared tool wall and repair stands during open hours. |
| SRC-003 | Parts are sold at cost; labor and teaching are free for members. |
| SRC-004 | New members join by attending one open-hours session. |

## Tone words from the client

Energetic, gritty, inclusive, empowering.

## What the director decides at Gate 01

1. **Who is it for?**
2. **What should they feel?**
3. **What should they do next?**

The room then adds three exclusions: things this spot will never do.
