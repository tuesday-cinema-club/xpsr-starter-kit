# Client Brief A: Ember & Oak Coffee

> Workshop practice client. Ember & Oak is a fictional business written for this exercise.

## The ask

Ember & Oak is a neighborhood micro-roaster opening its second shop. They want a **30-second launch spot** for social media and the shop's window screen.

## Client fact sheet

Treat these as the only facts you can claim. Each one is already in the demo research log (`examples/ember-and-oak-demo/research_log.csv`) with an id you can cite.

| Id | Fact |
| --- | --- |
| SRC-001 | Ember & Oak roasts in small batches in the back room of its original shop. |
| SRC-002 | The second shop is opening in the same neighborhood, a short walk from the first. |
| SRC-003 | Every bag is labelled with the roast date. |
| SRC-004 | The founder buys green coffee from two importers who publish their farm partners. |

## Tone words from the client

Grounded, warm, communal, made by hand.

## What the director decides at Gate 01

1. **Who is it for?**
2. **What should they feel?**
3. **What should they do next?**

The room then adds three exclusions: things this spot will never do.
